# Demo asset rights policy

No asset may enter a public CultureShift demo without known provenance and explicit rights information. Repository presence does not imply permission to reproduce, distribute, adapt, create derivatives, or display an asset publicly.

Third-party rights remain third-party rights. CultureShift does not relicense them, and applicable notices must be preserved in accordance with `LICENSE_SCOPE.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Unclear ownership, licensing, permitted use, derivative-work permission, public-display permission, or attribution requirements fail closed.

Private, customer, and user-provided assets must never be promoted into demo assets. Access for private processing is distinct from permission for public demonstration.

Synthetic assets must be marked synthetic and must identify their creator/owner status. Synthetic status does not eliminate rights in incorporated source material and is not evidence of real people, markets, audience behavior, or cultural findings.

Attribution requirements and required wording must remain attached to the asset record and be preserved wherever use is permitted. The manifest validator checks explicit metadata; it does not infer ownership or copyright permission and does not replace human rights review.
