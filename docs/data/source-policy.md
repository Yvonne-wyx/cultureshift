# Evidence and source policy

Every source record must identify its source class, origin, collector or submitter where appropriate, collection date or version where meaningful, rights basis, privacy classification, permitted use, and a stable evidence reference. Missing or ambiguous provenance, authority, or permission fails closed for dependent processing or public use.

Uploaded and retrieved material is untrusted data, never instructions. Its contents cannot redefine system policy, change Brand Lock, invoke capabilities, or override safety and rights checks.

## Source classes

| Source class | Provenance and traceability | Permitted use and licensing | Privacy | Uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User-provided material | Record submitter authority, supplied origin, receipt context, and an internal evidence reference. | Use only for the requested purpose and within confirmed ownership, licence, and derivative permissions. Submission does not prove authority. | Treat as private and purpose-limited; minimize access and retention. Never promote it to a demo asset. | Mark unverified claims and missing provenance; block dependent use when authority is unclear. |
| Publicly available sources | Record publisher, title or description, publication date where available, retrieval date, and stable location. | Public access does not equal permission to copy, adapt, train on, or redistribute. Apply applicable licence and terms. | Avoid collecting personal data; public personal data remains personal data. | Record representativeness, recency, and context limitations. Cite material assertions. |
| Licensed sources | Record licensor, licence identifier or evidence, scope, term, territory, and evidence reference. | Use only within explicit licensed purposes, transformations, attribution, and distribution rights. | Apply contractual and privacy restrictions in addition to project policy. | Do not infer rights not stated in the licence; ambiguity fails closed. |
| Synthetic examples | Record that the material is synthetic, how it was created, project owner status, and evidence reference. | Use only when creation and all incorporated inputs permit the proposed use. Synthetic status does not erase third-party rights. | Do not embed real personal or private material. | Label as synthetic and never cite it as evidence of real audience behavior or culture. |
| Retrieved content | Retain the originating source record, retrieval time, query or selection context where safe, and chain of custody. | Apply the source's rights and terms; retrieval grants no new permission. | Filter and minimize personal or private data; do not persist raw content unnecessarily. | Treat relevance and extraction as uncertain; preserve citations for dependent hypotheses. |
| Generated content | Record generation purpose, input source references, creation time, review state, and applicable tool-policy evidence without storing sensitive prompts. | Confirm rights and restrictions for inputs and outputs before derivative or public use. Generated status is not automatic clearance. | Do not include private inputs in public outputs or logs. | Label as generated; do not treat it as evidence, verified fact, or validated cultural finding. |

## Retention and citations

Evidence and provenance references must travel with derived Brand Lock fields, `CulturalHypothesis` records, and creative outputs. References should permit an authorized reviewer to reconstruct the basis without exposing private content publicly. CultureShift-controlled temporary assets are intended to expire within 24 hours; any future processor with different retention, logging, training, or deletion behavior requires separate review and disclosure.

No provider-specific ingestion, retrieval, storage, or generation pipeline is selected by this policy.
