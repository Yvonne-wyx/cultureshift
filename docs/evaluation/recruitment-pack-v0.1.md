# CultureShift Reviewer Recruitment Pack v0.1

## Truthful status

Materials are ready for human activation. No outreach, reviewer, consent, or
response is claimed by this repository. A named human coordinator must establish
an approved private contact route and protected consent vault before activation.

## Purpose and scope

The study asks adult reviewers to compare two blinded static advertisements for
AI software/apps in one supported direction. It collects bounded reviewer
observations about pending hypotheses; it does not ask anyone to validate a
country or culture.

## Eligibility and exclusions

Eligible participants are adult reviewers with relevant language or
target-context experience through living, advertising, design, product, or
closely related work. Nationality is not an eligibility requirement. Exclude a
person from a case when they created it, have an undisclosed conflict, lack the
needed language comfort, or cannot give voluntary consent. Do not collect
protected characteristics merely to screen eligibility.

## What participation involves

Participation covers a bounded set of blinded A/B comparisons, six 1-5 criteria,
five severe-risk flags, an optional preference, and optional minimized rationale.
Expected duration, assignment count, any compensation, and the withdrawal
deadline must be set by the human coordinator before an invitation is sent.

## Consent scopes

Ask separately for participation, storage of raw responses, anonymized aggregate
reporting, public attribution, and quotation. Declining attribution or quotation
does not prevent private participation. Consent for anonymized aggregate
reporting is required before a score counts in public results or release gates.

## Privacy and contact boundary

Invitations direct interested reviewers to the approved private contact route,
not a public Issue. Identities, handles, contact details, consent, private
randomization keys, and raw responses remain in access-controlled human-owned
storage and never enter Git or AI context. The coordinator supplies a complaint
route, retention deadline, deletion procedure, and withdrawal process before
activation.

## Coordinator activation checklist

- Name the accountable human coordinator privately.
- Establish the approved private contact route and complaint route.
- Prepare the protected consent vault and random reviewer-ID mapping.
- Freeze expected duration, compensation terms if any, retention, deletion, and
  withdrawal deadline.
- Confirm the assignment plan can produce at least three independent ratings per
  case without using nationality as a proxy.
- Update only the non-identifying status after real outreach begins.
