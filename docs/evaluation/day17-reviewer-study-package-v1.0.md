# CultureShift Bilateral Reviewer Study Package v1.0

**Dated:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Preview materials only / recruitment not open
**Directions:** China → UK and UK → China

## Purpose and limits

This later study would compare two blinded static-ad variants for AI software
in each supported direction. It asks for bounded reviewer observations about
clarity, truthfulness, trust, CTA meaning, possible stereotyping, and perceived
cultural appropriateness. Reviewer observations are not objective cultural
truth. The study cannot grant legal, cultural, brand, production, campaign, or
commercial approval.

No real reviewer has been confirmed, no response has been collected, and no
finding has been produced. Compensation, recruitment, retention, and activation
remain unapproved.

## Inclusion criteria

Participants must be adults able to give voluntary informed consent. For the
assigned direction they should have relevant target-language comfort and
target-context experience gained through living, advertising, design, product,
communications, or closely related work. Nationality is neither required nor a
proxy for cultural knowledge.

## Exclusion criteria

Exclude a person from a case if they created either variant, have an undisclosed
conflict, lack sufficient language comfort, cannot consent voluntarily, or are
currently distressed by the material. Do not screen for unnecessary protected
characteristics. CultureShift staff or contractors may not be presented as
independent reviewers of their own work.

## Participant information

Before consent, provide the study purpose, both task directions, number of
comparisons, expected duration, foreseeable discomfort, optional questions,
compensation terms, privacy handling, retention deadline, withdrawal deadline,
contact route, complaint route, and the limitation that de-identified aggregate
results may no longer permit removal of one contribution after the stated
aggregate freeze.

## Consent language

“I have read the participant information, am at least 18, understand that
participation is voluntary, may skip any question, and may withdraw through the
stated private route before the aggregate-freeze deadline. I understand that my
ratings are observations rather than cultural facts or professional legal
approval.”

Record separate choices for participation, raw-response storage, de-identified
aggregate reporting, attributed quotation, and anonymous quotation. Declining
attribution or quotation must not prevent otherwise eligible participation.

## Withdrawal procedure

The participant uses the approved private contact and withdrawal route, quotes
only their private study code, and need not give a reason. The withdrawal owner
confirms receipt, blocks unused assignments, and deletes or excludes the linked
response before the published deadline. The public repository never contains
the mapping between identity and study code.

## Privacy and data minimisation

Collect only study code, consent scopes, direction, counterbalance assignment,
bounded ratings/flags, preference, minimized optional rationale, timestamps
needed for ordering, and withdrawal state. Keep contact/identity data in a
separate access-controlled store. Do not collect nationality, precise location,
private client materials, account credentials, raw prompts, or unrelated
demographics. Do not commit identities, consent records, assignments, or raw
responses to Git or AI context.

Data-retention period: **not yet approved**. Retention and deletion must be
approved before recruitment.

## Screening questions

1. Are you 18 or older and able to consent voluntarily?
2. Which direction can you review: China → UK, UK → China, or both?
3. How comfortable are you reading the target language for that direction?
4. What relevant target-context, advertising, design, product, or communications experience do you have? (bounded categories plus “prefer not to say”)
5. Did you create, commission, or directly review either variant?
6. Do you have a conflict of interest that could affect this comparison?
7. Do you need an accessibility adjustment to participate?

## Bilateral task instructions

Each eligible reviewer sees assignments for China → UK or UK → China, never an
unsupported direction. For each case, read the authorized source, verified
facts, Brand Lock, warnings, and limitations. Inspect blinded Variant A and
Variant B. First assess factual accuracy and Brand Lock preservation; a failure
blocks overall preference. Then rate the remaining criteria, flag possible
harm, choose an optional overall preference, and provide a bounded reason for
preference. Do not infer market performance or speak for an entire culture.

## Blinded and counterbalanced presentation

Hide “CultureShift,” control/candidate, Version 1/Version 2, system/provider,
author, and prompt labels from reviewers where doing so does not remove required
provenance or safety context. Generate A/B labels from a private assignment
table outside Git. Within each direction, use an AB/BA counterbalanced schedule
so left/right and first/second order differ as evenly as the final sample allows.
Analyze China → UK and UK → China separately. Researchers retain the key only
for integrity checks and unblinding after the preregistered freeze.

## Rating rubric

Use 1 (strong concern), 2 (material concern), 3 (uncertain or mixed), 4
(generally suitable for further review), and 5 (strongly suitable for further
review), plus “cannot assess.” Each rating is a reviewer observation.

- **Clarity and readability:** language, hierarchy, and immediate comprehension.
- **Factual accuracy:** consistency with supplied verified product facts.
- **Brand Lock preservation:** Logo, product name, facts, real UI, benefit order, CTA action meaning, and layout.
- **Perceived cultural appropriateness:** suitability in the reviewer’s observed context, not cultural truth.
- **Possible stereotyping:** whether framing may flatten, exoticize, or generalize people or practices.
- **Trust and credibility:** whether evidence, uncertainty, and claims feel appropriately bounded.
- **CTA comprehension:** what action the CTA asks the viewer to take.
- **Overall preference:** A, B, tie, neither, or cannot assess, only after the blocking checks.
- **Reason for preference:** one minimized optional explanation tied to the rubric.

Record separate severe-risk flags for Brand Lock failure, unsupported claim,
privacy/rights concern, prohibited category, harmful or misleading content,
possible stereotype, and material discomfort.

## Qualitative follow-up questions

1. What was easiest and hardest to understand?
2. Which claim or visual most affected trust, and why?
3. What, if anything, felt culturally inappropriate or stereotyped in your observed context?
4. What action did you understand the CTA to request?
5. What single change would make the preferred variant safer or clearer?
6. Is there anything you could not assess from the supplied evidence?

## Adverse content and discomfort

Reviewers may pause, skip, or stop without penalty. The interface or facilitator
must provide a visible stop route and must not require a detailed explanation.
Flag potentially harmful, discriminatory, privacy-invasive, or prohibited
content without asking the reviewer to reproduce it in free text. Preserve the
minimum evidence needed for escalation and restrict access.

## Researcher escalation procedure

Pause the affected case; do not show it to further reviewers. Notify the named
research owner and adverse-event escalation owner through the approved private
route. For rights/privacy concerns, isolate the asset. For possible harm or
stereotyping, obtain accountable human review, including the approved cultural
expert role where applicable. Document only bounded, non-identifying decisions.
Do not reinterpret an escalation as approval.

## Stopping rules

Stop a case immediately for a Brand Lock failure, unsupported factual claim,
prohibited category, rights/privacy concern, credible harm, material reviewer
distress, broken blinding, or corrupted assignment. Stop the whole study if
consent, withdrawal, access control, randomization integrity, or the approved
contact/escalation route fails. Resume only after the research owner documents
correction and reapproval; never reuse withdrawn data.

## Compensation and recruitment placeholders

- Compensation: **not yet approved**; amount, method, timing, and budget owner are required.
- Recruitment channel: **not yet approved**; no invitation or outreach is authorized.
- Data retention: **not yet approved**; duration, deletion owner, and aggregate-freeze date are required.

## Decision rule

Analyze each direction separately. Exclude withdrawn or non-consented responses
and block any case with Brand Lock or severe-risk failure. Report rating
distributions, preference counts, reasons, missingness, and order balance
descriptively; do not claim statistical significance without a separately
approved analysis plan. A candidate may advance only if its Brand Lock passes,
no unresolved severe-risk flag remains, and its bounded reviewer observations
do not show a material clarity, factual, trust, CTA, cultural-appropriateness, or
stereotyping concern. Mixed or insufficient evidence remains unresolved and
requires accountable human judgment.

## Limitations

The planned convenience sample cannot represent a country, language community,
or culture. Blinding may be imperfect, observations are context-specific, and
ratings do not establish accessibility, legality, cultural correctness,
campaign performance, or commercial suitability. Even favorable results remain
inputs to later human review, not approval.
