# English-to-Chinese (mainland China) localization rule card v1

**Scope:** static ads for AI software/apps localized from the UK for a mainland China context. This is an operational drafting aid, not legal advice or evidence of cultural acceptance.

## Non-negotiable boundary

Brand Lock remains unchanged: **logo**, **product name**, **verified product facts**, **real UI**, **benefit order**, **CTA action meaning**, and **layout template**. Only narrative, use scenario, verified trust information, and language may be proposed for localization. Human review is required before use.

## Source-backed rules

These rules follow directly from the cited source. They are compliance or language-use constraints, not claims about what all Chinese audiences prefer.

| ID | Rule for the Chinese draft | Basis and limit | Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EZC-S1 | Use standard Chinese characters for ad copy intended for mainland China; do not assume an English-only rendering is adequate where Chinese is required. | The National Common Language and Script Law identifies Putonghua and standard Chinese characters as the national common language/script and addresses standard characters in advertising. Exact application requires legal review. [S1] | Native-language proofread plus channel-specific legal review. |
| EZC-S2 | Keep the internet ad truthful and lawful, and preserve any information that law, regulation, mandatory standard, or other rule requires to be displayed or disclosed. | The 2023 Measures for the Administration of Internet Advertising apply to commercial ads distributed via sites, pages, and apps in China. [S2] | Regulatory checklist for the intended channel; do not hide required information. |
| EZC-S3 | Keep product function, performance, origin, use, quality, price, producer, validity period, promises, and other objective statements true, accurate, clear, and consistent with verified evidence. | The effective Advertising Law (2021 revision) governs ad truthfulness and objective product statements. [S3] | Claim-by-claim evidence mapping; block unsupported or strengthened translations. |
| EZC-S4 | If cited data, surveys, statistics, quotations, or other referenced material appears in the ad, retain a truthful and accurate source and applicable scope/validity information. | The Advertising Law contains requirements for referenced content; exact presentation needs qualified legal review. [S3] | Citation and validity-date check; preserve source provenance. |

## Hypotheses requiring validation

Every item below is a `CulturalHypothesis`, not a rule or fact. Do not apply it by default.

| ID | CulturalHypothesis | Why uncertain | Required validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EZC-H1 | A concrete workflow outcome may be easier to understand than a literal translation of an idiomatic English headline. | This is a translation/comprehension hypothesis, not evidence of a nationwide preference. | Native-speaker comprehension interviews for the defined segment; test literal and adapted variants. |
| EZC-H2 | Explaining an unfamiliar English technical term in concise Chinese on first use may reduce ambiguity. | Familiarity differs by occupation, age, channel, and product category. | Terminology research with the target segment and product subject-matter review. |
| EZC-H3 | Placing verified privacy, data-handling, or product-capability evidence near the related claim may strengthen comprehension or trust. | The relevant cue and acceptable density vary; no source here establishes causal uplift. | Verify the fact, then conduct target-context comprehension and credibility testing. |

Record each adopted hypothesis with target segment, evidence references, uncertainty, alternatives/counterevidence, validation requirement, and `pending` review status. Generation cannot mark it approved.

## Release gate

Fail closed if product evidence, rights, provenance, target segment, Brand Lock, or required human/legal validation is missing or conflicted. Do not claim cultural correctness, legal compliance, or performance uplift.
