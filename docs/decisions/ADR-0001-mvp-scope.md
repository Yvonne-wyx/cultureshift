# ADR-0001: MVP scope

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Implementation needs a narrow boundary that can be evaluated without implying support for other formats, products, directions, or sensitive categories.

## Decision

The MVP supports only static advertising creatives for AI software/apps in the China to UK and UK to China directions. Politics, medical, financial, gambling, tobacco, and child-targeted advertising are excluded.

## Consequences

Unsupported formats, categories, products, and directions fail closed. Scope expansion requires a later explicit decision and corresponding safety review.
