# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project environment. Participants should engage with care across differences in culture, language, identity, experience, and viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Separate evidence from interpretation, especially in cultural discussions.
- Treat cultural claims as hypotheses open to review.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Accept correction and prioritize the safety of affected people.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public Issue or pull request. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary and will handle reports as confidentially as reasonably possible.
